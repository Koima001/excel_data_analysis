## Findings and Recommendations

### 1. Industrial Logistics Analysis

#### Key Findings

**Material Cost Analysis**
- Industrial Cleaning Supplies recorded the highest material cost at approximately **KES 93 million**, followed closely by Construction Additives at approximately **KES 92 million**.
- Maintenance Parts accounted for approximately KES 80 million, while Packaging Materials recorded approximately KES 77 million.
- Water Treatment Chemicals and Protective Coatings recorded relatively lower costs of approximately KES 74 million each.
- The difference between the highest and lowest material costs was approximately KES 19 million.

**Customer Cost Analysis**
- Lake Region Water Services recorded the highest customer expenditure at approximately **KES 74 million**.
- Rift Valley Manufacturing followed at approximately KES 67 million, while BlueWave Facilities recorded approximately KES 65 million.
- Highland Agro Processors recorded the lowest expenditure at approximately KES 47 million.
- The expenditure difference between the highest and lowest customers was approximately KES 27 million.

**Depot Inventory Analysis**
- Mombasa Depot recorded approximately 60,000 units of Maintenance Parts, the highest quantity for this material category.
- Nakuru Depot recorded approximately 59,000 units of Industrial Cleaning Supplies, exceeding the quantities recorded by the other depots.
- Material quantities varied considerably across depots, indicating differences in inventory distribution and potential demand patterns.

**Monthly Cost Trends**
- April and October recorded some of the highest monthly costs, at approximately KES 51 million and KES 48 million, respectively.
- February, March, June, and November recorded comparatively lower monthly costs of approximately KES 33 million.
- Monthly expenditure fluctuated throughout the year, suggesting variations in material demand, purchasing patterns or operational requirements.

#### Recommendations

- Review procurement costs for Industrial Cleaning Supplies and Construction Additives, which collectively accounted for approximately KES 185 million in expenditure.
- Investigate the expenditure patterns of Lake Region Water Services and other high-cost customers to identify opportunities for improved cost management.
- Optimize inventory distribution across depots based on demand, consumption patterns and storage capacity.
- Investigate monthly expenditure peaks to improve procurement scheduling and budget forecasting.
- Establish regular monitoring of material costs and customer expenditure to identify cost-saving opportunities.

### 2. Production Operations Analysis

#### Key Findings

**Production Cost Analysis**
- Industrial Cleaners recorded the highest product cost at approximately KES 12.5 million.
- Packaging Resin followed at approximately KES 12.3 million, while Water Treatment Chemicals recorded approximately KES 11.7 million.
- Construction Additives recorded the lowest product cost at approximately KES 10 million.
- Production expenditure varied across plants and products, with Mombasa Plant recording relatively high expenditure on Industrial Cleaners and Athi River Plant recording high expenditure on Protective Coatings.

**Production Target Achievement**
- Total production target: **752,080 units**
- Actual units produced: **719,038 units**
- Production shortfall: **33,042 units**
- Target achievement rate: **95.61%**
- Target shortfall: **4.39%**

The production results indicate that the organization achieved approximately 96% of its production target but did not fully meet the planned output.

**Quality Performance**
- Accepted units: **705,614**
- Rejected units: **13,424**
- Total units: **719,038**
- Acceptance rate: **98.13%**
- Rejection rate: **1.87%**

Although the acceptance rate was relatively high, rejected products accounted for 13,424 units, representing an opportunity to reduce production waste and improve quality.

**Monthly Production Trends**
- October recorded the highest monthly production at approximately 77,000 units.
- February recorded approximately 74,000 units, followed by June at approximately 70,000 units.
- May recorded the lowest monthly production at approximately 45,000 units.
- Production fluctuated throughout the year, indicating possible variations in production demand, capacity utilization or operational performance.

**Operational Performance**
- Operating hours were approximately 1,400–1,500 hours across the three plants.
- Downtime was approximately 200 hours per plant.
- Athi River Plant recorded approximately 1,480 operating hours, while Mombasa Plant recorded approximately 1,420 hours.
- Similar downtime levels across plants indicate the importance of monitoring equipment reliability and maintenance performance.

#### Recommendations

- Investigate the production shortfall of 33,042 units to identify operational bottlenecks and improve target achievement.
- Review production costs for Industrial Cleaners and Packaging Resin to identify opportunities for reducing manufacturing expenses.
- Investigate the 13,424 rejected units and identify recurring quality issues to improve production processes and reduce waste.
- Strengthen preventive maintenance practices to minimize downtime and improve equipment reliability.
- Investigate monthly production fluctuations to improve production scheduling and resource allocation.
- Monitor production targets, rejection rates, operating hours and downtime regularly to support operational decision-making.
